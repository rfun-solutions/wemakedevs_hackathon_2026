# Access promises

Five promises that Kay, a conference attendee who needs access features, can hold us to. Each has one sentence in EN and ES, the rules that protect it, and the live signal behind its status light. Rules cite the ids (`IN-2 (Promise P4)`). Strings: `access.promise.P1` to `P5` in `web/src/i18n/{en,es}/access.json`; code: `web/src/features/access/promises.ts`.

A light is never green without data. No samples means **unknown** (grey, "not measured right now").

Spanish sentences are Claude-assisted and have not had native review (rule IN-9, `AI-DISCLOSURE.md`).

## P1
- EN: Captions reach your screen within 4 seconds, or we switch and tell you.
- ES: Los subtítulos llegan a tu pantalla en menos de 4 segundos, o cambiamos de sistema y te avisamos.
- Rules: IN-3, IN-7, IN-10, CAP-1 (generated by G)
- Signal: `kc_captions_segment_ms` p95 of the faster engine, limit 4000 ms
- Unknown: no caption segments in the window, so the light is grey ("not measured right now").

## P2
- EN: A note saves in under half a second, or waits safely on your phone and saves later.
- ES: Una nota se guarda en menos de medio segundo, o espera segura en tu teléfono y se guarda después.
- Rules: IN-11 (plus G's generated HP rule when `POST /api/notes` is hot)
- Signal: `POST /api/notes` p95 from span metrics, limit 500 ms
- Unknown: no note saves in the window, so the light is grey ("not measured right now").

## P3
- EN: Every button and screen works with text at 200% and in high contrast.
- ES: Todos los botones y pantallas funcionan con el texto al 200 % y con alto contraste.
- Rules: A11Y-1, A11Y-2, IN-4, IN-5
- Signal: Synthetic Kay (200% type, high contrast) axe result, `kc_status_kay_checks_total{promise="P3"}` over 30 min
- Unknown: no Synthetic Kay result in 30 minutes, so the light is grey ("not measured right now").

## P4
